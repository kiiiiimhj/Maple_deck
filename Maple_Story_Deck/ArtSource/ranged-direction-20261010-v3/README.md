# Directional projectiles v3

Built-in image generation edit mode: newly drawn ice-side and thorn-front, with reused ice-front and thorn-side. Four Yeti and three Nepenthes species select frontal or oblique art at launch. Leftward shots mirror the sprite and keep the same upright orientation as rightward shots. Art is selected once per shot to avoid swapping mid-flight. Original timing, muzzle coordinates, damage, endpoint region, size and dark tints are retained.

## Final prompts

### ice-side

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image is the edit target: existing three-shard cyan ice projectile. Produce a matching THREE-QUARTER SIDE/DEPTH VIEW variant for attacks travelling from screen-left toward the camera and screen-right. Same exact kind of three faceted ice shards, same MapleStory-inspired clean cartoon illustration, dark blue outlines, cyan/white crystals and restrained frosty white wake. Dominant central sharp crystal spear, two smaller flanking crystals. The lead crystal points to the RIGHT, exactly horizontally; its point is at the right of the composition and wake trails to the LEFT. Not an arrow aiming down and not a vertical frontal cluster. We can see both the broad front-facing crystalline facets AND its length receding in depth: viewing angle roughly 45 degrees off head-on, foreshortened, not a flat side profile. Keep the head big and the tail short, similar visual mass to the reference sprite. The three shards read as the same cluster from another camera angle, not a new ice weapon. Center full cluster with transparent padding. Genuine transparent background, no text, no UI, no scene, no particles detached far from the silhouette.

### thorn-front

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image is the edit target: green thorn-covered seed projectile. Create its FRONTAL DEPTH VIEW for a first-person game attack coming straight OUT OF THE SCREEN toward the player. Preserve the very same organic green seed, brown woody thorn casing and bright lime-green pointed thorns, same clean MapleStory-inspired cartoon game style, dark outlines and illustrated highlights. Render a compact foreshortened seed: large central convex green front face, short pointed lime tip near the center front, brown curved thorn casing around the spherical seed, a few smaller thorns receding behind. It should be recognizable as the input projectile seen from the front instead of a long sideways spear. Short subtler rear streaks only peeking behind silhouette, not a long upper-left tail. Near-round compact silhouette, front and depth facets visible, complete centered object and transparent margins. Real transparent background, no text, no scene, no character, no extra objects, no border.
